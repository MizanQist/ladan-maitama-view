The enclave

Plot 758 · CAD Zone A05 · Maitama

Nine homes behind one gate.

Maitama View is a gated development of nine homes on Plot 758, CAD Zone A05, Maitama District: five detached duplexes along the eastern boundary and two pairs of semi-detached houses on the west, set along a private, palm-lined avenue with a planted island at its heart. A single gate on the access road, with the gatehouse and generator house beside it, is the only way in.

Only 28 per cent of the ground is built on. The rest is paving, lawn and planting: a three-metre band of garden inside the boundary wall, twenty-three palms, fifty-four trees and over a thousand shrubs. Your block is the second pair in from the gate, on the island side of the avenue, with the largest detached house beyond it.

The avenue is the address.

From the gate, the avenue runs the length of the site between the planted band on one side and the row of detached houses on the other. Every home has a paved plot of its own with parking at the door, beneath the oversailing upper floors, and the avenue's island of palms and trees sits directly in front of your block.

The gatehouse at the entrance has a guard room, kitchenette and WC, with the generator house behind a fire-rated steel door. The boundary wall is a 900 mm sancrete wall with concrete infill between reinforced-concrete columns, a hollow-section steel railing above and precast copings throughout.

Your block

SB2 · semi-detached pair · both homes

Two homes under one roof.

SB2 is a pair of mirrored houses about a party wall, and both halves are yours. Each home is 7.5 by 12 metres at ground, growing to 8.7 by 13.9 metres on the two bedroom floors above, with four en-suite bedrooms, a family living room, a balcony on each upper floor and parking at its own front door.

Structure & envelope

Frame
Reinforced-concrete frame on pad foundations at −1.5 m; 200 mm in-situ ground slab; 4.05 m ground to first floor, 3.6 m floor to floor above, the upper floors oversailing the ground-floor walls
Walls
230 mm blockwork, 12 mm plaster-rendered, primed and finished with two coats of emulsion to a RAL-approved colour; travertine-faced piers; the balcony screens in a dark perforated panel
Roof
150 mm reinforced-concrete roof cover to the semi-detached houses, behind a parapet capped in 0.7 mm colour-coated aluminium at +12.45 m
Curtain walls
Powder-coated aluminium curtain walling on 1.5 mm NIGALEX profiles, glazed in 6 mm tinted toughened safety glass to the living room and the second-floor front

Doors & windows

Entrance
Single-leaf steel security pivot door with reinforced leaf and frame, multi-point locking, tamper-proof hinges and weather seals, to Israeli standard or approved equivalent
Terrace
Three-leaf sliding glass doors in powder-coated aluminium, 6 mm tinted toughened glass, with concealed track, floor guide and soft-close
Internal
38 mm solid-core flush doors in polished teak veneer on 40 × 90 mm hardwood frames, fire-rated E60 with intumescent and smoke seals
Windows
Outward-swing awning and triple-leaf casement windows in white powder-coated aluminium, 6 mm clear or tinted toughened glass, aluminium lever handles and friction stays

Finishes & rooms

Floors
10 mm vitrified floor tiles on a 50 mm cement screed throughout, over the 200 mm ground slab and the upper-floor slabs
Kitchen
Fitted kitchen with island unit to the joinery details in the drawing set, with a store beside it
Bedrooms
Every bedroom en suite; fitted wardrobes to the wardrobe details; a walk-in closet to the master suite; a visitors' WC at the entrance
Balconies
Glass balustrades; the first-floor balcony behind the perforated screen, the second-floor balcony beneath the roof overhang
